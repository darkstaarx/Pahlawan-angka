# Pahlawan Angka v3.8.30

## Latihan Fokus Penjaga

- Parent Dashboard can launch a 5, 10, or 15 question practice locked to one current-grade skill.
- The normal adaptive route remains unchanged outside a focus session.
- Cikgu Wajar now gives graduated hints, including a smaller-number example where appropriate.
- Correct-answer feedback explains why the answer works for core arithmetic forms.
- Each focus session stores a compact before/after report in `guardianFocusHistory`.
- The ambiguous Darjah 6 money wording now distinguishes spendable money from money placed in an unavailable savings pot.

## Scope preserved

Battle staging, heroes, pets, attacks, final blows, shop, achievements, Restu Penjaga, Dev Mode, and the PWA identity are unchanged.

## Evidence basis

The flow follows IES recommendations for systematic instruction, accessible numbers, visual/verbal support, worked examples, probing questions, and tailored feedback, plus EEF guidance that feedback should identify misconceptions and move learning forward.
