# v3.86.38 — Darjah 1 place value: no repeated digits, no give-away place

- Reported item: "Nilai digit 6 pada tempat sa dalam 66 ialah?" — 66 has two 6s, and naming the place made the answer a copy of the digit.
- Year 1 SP 1.6.1 (all modes) now uses two different digits and a non-zero ones digit.
- Year 1 digit-value item no longer names the place: "Apakah nilai digit 6 dalam 63?" (60) or "…digit 3 dalam 63?" (3); distractors follow face-value / swapped-place errors.
- Legacy D1.PV100 digit-value item: same change; never asks about a 0 digit.
- Shared place-value bank (all grades): "berada pada tempat?" now points at the marked digit, and its place-name choices are always place names (no "Tidak pasti" when the answer is sa). The marked digit-value prompt keeps its place name, as locked by `place-value-clarity-v3.14.5`.
