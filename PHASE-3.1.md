## Learning Coach v2
The intervention now teaches a solution process rather than displaying passive concept notes:
1. Faham — identify what the question asks.
2. Bina — Coach demonstrates a worked example step-by-step.
3. Sambung — learner completes an intermediate solution step.
4. Cuba — learner solves a fresh same-skill question independently.
5. Guna — learner transfers the method to another generated question.

Guided examples include place value, addition/subtraction regrouping, multiplication/division groups, fraction, decimal, money, time, measurement, shapes and data.
