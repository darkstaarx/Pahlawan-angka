# Research Notes — KSSR Matematik Tahun 6 Space + Data

## Unit 6: Ruang

The KSSR Semakan Tahun 6 standards reviewed for this repair specify:
- 6.1.1: regular polygons up to eight sides and measurement of interior angles;
- 6.1.2: forming an angle from a given value using appropriate geometry tools, with angles up to 180°;
- 6.2.1: identify centre, diameter and radius of a circle;
- 6.2.2: draw a circle from a given radius and label centre/radius/diameter;
- 6.3.1: solve daily problems involving space.

The previous `D6.AREA` rectangle/perimeter/cuboid-volume node therefore cannot serve as the Year 6 Space mastery contract.

## Unit 8: Pengurusan Data dan Kebolehjadian

The standards reviewed specify:
- 8.1.1: complete and interpret pie charts using sector information such as 45°, 90° and 180° with given quantities;
- 8.2.1: state whether an event may or may not occur and give a reasonable reason;
- 8.2.2: use qualitative chance language such as impossible, less likely, equally likely, more likely and certain, with reasonable reasoning;
- 8.3.1: solve daily problems involving data management and chance.

This makes the old numeric `prob_fraction` path unsuitable as Year 6 mastery evidence.
