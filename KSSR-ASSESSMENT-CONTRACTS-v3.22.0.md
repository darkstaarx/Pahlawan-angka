# KSSR Assessment Contracts — v3.22.0

## Non-negotiable rule

**A harder grade must test a different competency/demand, not merely use larger digits.**

Variation is allowed only inside the contract of the active skill. It must never replace the competency being evidenced for mastery.

## Grade progression

### Tahun 1 — concrete recognition and meaning
- Number: count, compare, order, missing number, simple number line.
- Place value: tens/ones, blocks, compose/decompose. **No rounding inside D1.PV100.**
- Operations: object/group model, simple story, symbolic.
- Fractions: one-half and quarters through equal-part visuals/sharing.
- Measurement: direct comparison of length/mass/capacity rather than unit arithmetic.
- Data: pictograph/count/most/difference with small values.

### Tahun 2 — preserved v3.18.1 skill contracts
D2 already has explicit repair by standards: number words, blocks, estimation of quantity distinct from rounding, number-line rounding, patterns, 2/3-number operations, ×10, division/remainder, money/time/measure/space/data subskills. v3.22.0 does not replace these with a broader generator.

### Tahun 3 — representation + connected concepts
- Fractions: fraction of a group, equivalent fractions, simplest form, addition/subtraction with compatible denominators.
- Decimals: place value, compare, fraction-decimal link, simple operations.
- Operations: exact/remainder, missing values, context.
- Data: read, total, difference, interpretation.
- Shape: perimeter and missing-side reasoning.

### Tahun 4 — transition to upper-primary assessment demand
- Fractions: improper ↔ mixed, add up to 3, subtract, mixed add/sub, fraction of quantity.
- Decimals: up to 3 decimal places; add/subtract, multiply/divide.
- Time: 24-hour notation, duration, timeline and larger time relationships.
- Data: multi-read and two-step questions.
- Perimeter: missing-side and simple composite reasoning.

### Tahun 5 — new operation classes, not inflated Tahun 4
- Fractions: **multiplication** involving proper/whole/mixed forms.
- Decimals: round to 3 d.p.; combined +/−; multiplication/division.
- Percent: mixed number / percent, >100%, inverse quantity.
- Time: day/hour, calendar/leap-year, fractional/decimal time conversions.
- Measurement: fractional/decimal unit conversion and arithmetic.
- Area: missing dimensions, comparison and composite rectangles.
- Coordinates: movement and grid-distance.
- Data: mean, missing value from mean, visual interpretation.

### Tahun 6 — reasoning and application
- Whole numbers: millions, prime/composite, patterns and reasonableness.
- Combined operations: brackets, unknowns, multi-step contexts, reasonableness.
- Fractions: **division** involving proper/whole/mixed forms.
- Decimals: decimal × decimal and decimal ÷ decimal.
- Percent: >100%, operations, inverse quantity.
- Ratio: simplest form, equivalent ratios, tables, proportional quantities, ratio from a total.
- Money: budget, multi-stage discount/cost, saving target.
- Time: **time zones** and two-step time-zone tasks.
- Measurement: relationship/multi-step contexts for length, mass and liquid volume.
- Coordinates: scaled distance, route, missing point and direction.
- Data: probability plus reasoning from charts/means.

## Diagram policy

Use clean KSSR-style instructional figures where the representation is part of the construct:
- fraction strips and sets
- hundred grids
- pictographs / bar charts / tables
- clocks / timelines
- base-10 blocks
- rulers / number lines
- geometry/composite diagrams
- coordinate maps with scale
- probability spinners/tables

Graphics are not decoration. A visual archetype must require the pupil to read the representation.

## Upper-primary response policy

Normal battle may still use choices for speed and game flow. However, Tahun 4–6 question construction should increasingly produce numeric/limited-response friendly answers so Boss Typed Answer can assess independent recall/application without multiple-choice cues.

## Known curriculum-graph boundary

The existing graph has `D5.AREA` and `D6.AREA` rather than full separate upper-primary Space nodes for every polygon/angle/circle competency. v3.22.0 **does not silently credit those unrelated competencies into AREA mastery**. It deepens the existing area/perimeter/volume contract only. If the product later wants full KSSR Space coverage, create explicit new skill nodes and migration/evidence rules first.
