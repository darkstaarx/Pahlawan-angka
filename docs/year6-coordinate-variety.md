# Year 6 coordinate map variety

KSSR Semakan 2017 Year 6 SP 7.1.1 requires horizontal and vertical distances between locations using a given scale. Coordinate reading and movement identify the locations; scaled-distance tasks retain the Year 6 focus.

Source: KPM DSKP Matematik Tahun 6, printed page 56, https://asiemodel.net/wp-content/uploads/2022/08/3.-DSKP-MATEMATIK-TAHUN-6_isbn.pdf

The active Unit 7 bank adds named-place map questions with coordinate, destination-name and labelled distance-pair answers. Each remains one independently scored question. Curriculum nodes, adaptive routing and mastery rules are unchanged.

Year 6 measurement prompts in the active bank and legacy fallback layers use complete Bahasa Melayu sentences while retaining the existing quantities and calculations.
