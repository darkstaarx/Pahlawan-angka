## Curriculum progression reviewed

The release uses the existing Pahlawan Angka skill graph as the mastery boundary, then aligns the internal archetypes to the year-level KSSR progression reviewed from the KSSR Semakan DSKP/teaching materials.

Key corrections implemented:
- Tahun 1 fractions remain equal-part concrete/visual concepts.
- Tahun 3 fractions add group/equivalent/simplest/compatible-denominator operations.
- Tahun 4 fractions move to improper/mixed forms and multi-term add/subtract.
- Tahun 5 fraction node now assesses multiplication rather than another same-denominator addition bank.
- Tahun 6 fraction node now assesses division rather than mixed→improper conversion.
- Tahun 5 decimals add rounding/×/÷.
- Tahun 6 decimals use decimal × decimal and decimal ÷ decimal.
- Tahun 5/6 percent includes >100% and inverse quantity.
- Tahun 6 time is shifted from speed-distance-time to time-zone reasoning.
- Tahun 6 ratio adds simplification and proportional quantity.
- Upper-primary questions add missing-value, multi-step, table/diagram and reasonableness archetypes.

No external copyrighted question is copied verbatim. The release implements original generated items from curriculum concepts and assessment structures.
