# Pahlawan Angka v3.15.1

## Topic worlds and restrained battle ambience

- Battle biasa now uses a quiet forest sound bed rather than foreground music.
- Boss encounters retain a controlled low musical layer and drum pulse.
- The battle arena changes with the current mathematics domain:
  - Number: forest temple
  - Operations: arithmetic forge
  - Fractions, decimals, percentages and ratios: crystal cave
  - Money: royal market
  - Time: clock-tower court
  - Measurement: measurement court
  - Shapes, space and coordinates: Nusantara temple
  - Data and probability: data observatory
- New terrain images share one horizon, open floor and character staging layout.
- All new terrain files are compressed WebP assets and included in the offline cache.
