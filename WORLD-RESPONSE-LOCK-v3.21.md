# Pahlawan Angka — World Response Lock v3.21

Status: **LOCKED PRODUCT PRINCIPLE**  
Baseline: live v3.20.0 at `512ebc2`.

## Core rule
**Matematik mesti menggerakkan dunia RPG.** World progression is a consequence of demonstrated learning, not a decorative reward economy.

## Proof gate
A skill may contribute to world restoration only when all are true:
- mastery >= 85%;
- confidence >= 70%;
- evidence >= 4;
- stability >= 55%;
- mandatory competency integrity is satisfied when the skill has explicit competency requirements.

XP, coins, login frequency, play duration, purchases, streaks and battle wins alone must never restore a landmark.

## World stages
0. **Masih sunyi** — no secure skill in that chapter.
1. **Mula menyala** — at least one skill is securely proven.
2. **Sedang dipulihkan** — at least half the chapter skills are securely proven.
3. **Pulih sepenuhnya** — every skill in the chapter is securely proven.

## Persistence rule
World restoration is a historical milestone and does not visually regress after a later isolated mistake. The mastery/adaptive engine remains free to mark a skill fragile and schedule review. Resetting the child profile resets world history naturally.

## Sensory intensity
- stage 1: low; ambient response only.
- stage 2: medium; landmark visibly comes alive.
- stage 3: high-but-brief reveal on Hub/Mission screens.
- never interrupt question reading, Cikgu Wajar teaching, boss entrance or final blow.

## Existing learner migration
On first v3.21 load, current proven stages become the starting baseline without retrospective celebration spam. Only later stage increases generate new world-change events.

## Do not change without a new product decision
- learning-evidence-only gate;
- monotonic world milestones;
- no coin/XP/streak path to restoration;
- no battle interruption for world reveals;
- competency-integrity requirement;
- reduced-motion support.
