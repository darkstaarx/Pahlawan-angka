# Pahlawan Angka v3.14.4

Adaptive battle ambience and boss score.

- Adds a low-volume procedural forest-wind ambience during normal battles.
- Crossfades into a darker tonal layer with restrained double-beat percussion when a boss appears.
- Fades the boss score out on defeat and stops all background audio outside the battle screen.
- Uses the existing sound toggle and mobile audio-unlock flow.
- Pauses background audio when the tab is hidden and avoids external or copyrighted music assets.
